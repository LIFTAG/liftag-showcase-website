# Regional boundary provenance

The simplified country outlines used by the LIFTAG gym discovery demo are adapted from Natural Earth, Admin 0 Countries, 1:10m cultural vectors. The source GeoJSON was retrieved from Natural Earth's maintained vector-data repository:

https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_countries.geojson

Natural Earth data is public domain. Source and terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The focal Czechia and Slovakia rings use a 0.065-degree simplification; surrounding context is reduced further for this illustrative view. The map is not intended for navigation or authoritative boundaries.
