# ASSEMBLY MECHANICS (doable — probed 2026-09-03)

Yes. Do not fake this with a vertex-shader explode of the fused GLB. That reads as a grenade, not a gym.

## What the pack actually is

Probed with Blender 5.2.1 on `all+fitness.blend`, object `3.003` (shipped hero):

| Fact | Number |
|---|---|
| Shipped `hero-machine.glb` | **One** mesh, 24,551 tris, 5 LIFTAG materials. Cannot animate parts until re-exported. |
| Loose CAD islands on `3.003` | **151** |
| Islands with ≥200 verts (real parts) | **25**, mostly left/right pairs (pads, rails, horns) |
| Islands with ≥50 verts | **37** |
| Islands with <20 verts (bolts, CAD junk) | **62** |
| Disc-like islands on the hero (weight plates) | **0**. Horns are empty. |
| Disc-like islands on pack `3.002` (`PlateRig` in `export_props.py`) | **6**, including a pair ~45 cm across, ~7.6 cm thick |

So: the machine **wants** to come apart (CAD shells joined into one object). Weight plates are **not on it**. They have to be extracted from `3.002` (or a 32-segment cylinder in LIFTAG_Steel) and **added** onto the horns.

## How to build it

1. **New exporter**, `tools/gym3d/export_hero_parts.py` (keep `export_hero.py` for the fused Act 1 mesh).
   - Split `3.003` by island.
   - Parent islands <50 verts onto the nearest ≥200-vert part (bolts do not fly).
   - Cluster the 25 large islands into **6–8 named groups**: `frame`, `pads`, `footplate`, `sled`, `horns`, `hardware`. Animate groups, not 151 shells.
   - Export `hero-machine-parts.glb`: root at the same rest pose as `hero-machine.glb`, children with local offsets of identity at rest.
   - Extract 2 plate meshes from `3.002` discs (or generate cylinders). Instance them. **Desktop: 2–4 plates per horn. Phone: 2 plates total.**
2. **Three.js, Act 0B only.** Parts start as hologram-aligned offsets (up + out from each group’s rest centroid, along the floor normal / horn axis). Stagger floor-up: frame plants (`drop.ts` may drive the frame group), pads lock, horns, then plates **slide on**. No cannon, no rigid-body solver.
3. **Swap to fused, keep the sled.** When 0B is planted, hide the parts graph and show existing `hero-machine.glb` plus plate instances. **Do not fuse the sled / footplate.** The static fused GLB omits those two islands, so the parts group is the only thing still holding them on screen. (It no longer travels — nothing does.) Act 1 lighting and hologram shell stay on the hero. Do not run `createHologramShell` on 25 children.
4. **Plates after LOG** stay geometrically ugly at seated eye (horn is behind the lens). Prefer plates as the **end of 0B** (wide shot, horns in frame). After LOG, the room answers with floor rings, not a reverse to the horn, unless you justify a phase change.

## Perf

The live path is fragment-bound at ~50k tris (machine + hologram shell), 17.7 ms worst at 1440×900 DPR 1.5. 8 part groups + 8 plate instances is still ~25k–35k tris. The risk is **draw calls + extra overdraw during explode**, not triangle count. If p99 breaks 20 ms, merge groups, cut plate count, skip neighbour machines. Do not raise DPR.

## What this is not

- Not a mesh explode / face shatter.
- Not 151 independently flying bolts.
- Not `gym-props.glb` as a warehouse.
- Not plates as WoW loot. Iron on steel. The QR sticker is the legendary object.
- Not lime on powder coat while parts travel. Wire copies may use the existing hologram front; solids use the planted PBR.

Feasibility child: map 0B to `export_hero_parts.py` + a parts rig in `stage.ts`. First prototype that proves the bet is **frame plants, pads lock, two plates slide on**, then fused swap. Not the full 25-island set.
