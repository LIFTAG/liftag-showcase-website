# Award submission working draft

**Project:** LIFTAG — Give your gym another dimension

A small equipment tag opens an entire training experience. The visitor watches a real leg press assemble, follows the NFC inlay and bending sticker onto the machine, and sees the gym fold into the LIFTAG phone. The product then becomes hands-on: log a demonstration set, revisit it, explore coaching, and connect real equipment to a gym catalog.

The visual language comes from LIFTAG's original gym-scan: near-black space, legible steel, precise lime accents, and continuously connected camera motion. Native scrolling, semantic controls, mobile compositions, still-image fallbacks, and the shared free-kit form keep the demonstration usable.

Technical details worth documenting after release: one renderer and render loop shared across the cinematic and equipment scenes; capability selection independent of composition; deferred product video and source-model loading; original material, tag, scanner and phone shaders; keyboard-operable demonstrations; resource disposal and frame-time-based resolution reduction.

Before submission, attach verified device results, production performance measurements, owner comprehension findings, and a full-quality browser recording. Do not describe a local preview or sampled capture as verified production or 120 Hz performance.
